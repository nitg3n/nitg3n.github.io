Project Default06 / The revenue model

High-value sponsorships.
Individually negotiated.

A small number of large contracts, negotiated directly with advertisers.

Slot feeThe contracted price for sponsor placement against an agreed audience and campaign scope.
Performance feeWhere agreed, an additional payment for signups, purchases, or another contracted outcome.
Contract termsTargeting, delivery, pricing, credited outcomes, verification, and payment timing are agreed individually.
Proposed model · High-value contract, not high-value purchase06 / 13
Project Default07 / The economics to validate

Sponsorship revenue
funds AI access.

First, sponsorship must cover the cost of serving every user. The remaining margin must fund development, sales, and overhead.

Costs / all users

AI inference

+

Tools, infrastructure, and user support

Cash sets operating capacity.

Collected sponsor payments and investment fund usage. Signed contract value is not cash; investment is not revenue.

Allowances bound serving costs.

Users choose the model. Higher-cost calls consume the allowance faster, including for users who never convert.

Project Default09 / The path to launch

A focused pilot.
A much bigger sponsorship business.

BEFORE OPENING

Working demo

Complete the tool and demonstrate sponsor delivery. Agree campaign measures with pilot advertisers; secure funds before enrollment.

SEVEN DAYS LIVE

Pilot data

Record active usage, task completion, sponsor exposure, acceptance, and verified contract outcomes alongside serving costs.

BEFORE FULL LAUNCH

Larger contracts

Use results to negotiate major cross-industry sponsorships. Test pricing and payment terms against the measured operating cost.

Seven days can establish delivery and early response, not retention or recurring demand. Contractual attribution supports settlement; it does not prove causal lift.
